As we approach the end of the year, we want to take a moment to thank you for a wonderful year! We truly appreciate each and every one of you.

It's been an eventful year. Our Broadmead team is thriving and our Duncan location continues to grow!

Black Friday is just around the corner!

November 25th to December 2nd we have 20% off all Aveda and Kérastase products! (Excluding gift packs, and price will be applied at checkout.)

We have always been a salon that strives to give back. We supported both local and international charities this year. In April we raised money for our Earth Month charity Mary's Meals, Ricardo donated his hair to Wigs for Kids and the salon raised money for the Cops for Cancer Tour de Rock! We want to finish off our year of giving in a personal way. Starting November 1st to December 15th, if you bring in a new, unwrapped toy, you'll receive a complimentary hair treatment as our thank you for supporting the Zone's toy drive in memory of the beloved Victoria radio host, Dylan Willows.

This year has brought many changes, and we are grateful for your patience and support throughout the process.

Wishing you all a wonderful holiday season, and we look forward to seeing you in the new year!

Warm regards,
Carreiro
