❋ Our story · Est. 2005

a salon, spa, barbershop and academy

we are a hair salon on paper

We assure you, we are much, much more… a salon, spa, barbershop and academy. Most of all, a place to relax and unwind. Inspired by European spas that honour your body, Carreiro pays tribute to your hair. Our talented hair artists are dedicated to making your visit an experience.

Since 2005, our goal has been to have you forget the outside world, well… leave it outside at least. Sleek and sophisticated, our studios provide the perfect atmosphere for creativity and personal excellence.

❋ Two studios

Broadmead & Duncan

Today you’ll find us in Broadmead, at 410-777 Royal Oak Drive in Saanich, and on Craig Street in downtown Duncan. Our studio artists, from the most seasoned stylists to new talent, are mentored to be the best in the profession.

❋ Giving back

we have always been a salon that strives to give back

We support both local and international charities. We have raised money for our Earth Month charity Mary’s Meals, our team has donated hair to Wigs for Kids, and the salon has raised money for the Cops for Cancer Tour de Rock.

Every holiday season we collect new, unwrapped toys for The Zone radio’s Willows Family Memorial Toy Drive, in memory of the beloved Victoria radio host Dylan Willows. Every guest who brings in a toy receives a complimentary deep conditioning treatment as our thank you.

We also ride in The Distinguished Gentleman’s Ride, raising funds and awareness for prostate cancer and men’s mental health on behalf of Movember. Read more →
